The dirty tree implements the R9 contract. A teacher can log in, page a Prisma-filtered 待审 queue, enter / review / promote, manage taxonomy and keys. Agents stay at `REVIEWED`. 1. `PATCH /api/suggestions/:id` accept returns `draft.updatedAt` plus `knowledgePointIds` / `difficulty` / `tagIds`.
